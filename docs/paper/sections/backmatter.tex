\section*{Artifact availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Artifact availability}

The Coq development, the documentation and every script used in this paper are public at
\url{https://github.com/blackwell-systems/normalization-confluence}; the paper cites the state of the
repository at tag \texttt{paper-v4}. To rebuild and check the
proofs:
\begin{center}
\texttt{cd coq \&\& make \&\& ./verify.sh}
\end{center}
\texttt{verify.sh} prints the assumptions of every gated result and fails unless each is closed under the
global context and their number reaches the gate (4{,}797). Continuous integration runs the same
command on Coq~8.18, Coq~8.20 and Rocq~9.3. The P2 search and its tests are in
\path{research/predictions/p2/}; the P3 models, harnesses and result files are in
\path{research/predictions/route2/}; the replicator scenarios are in
\path{research/couchdb-replicator/}; the committed predictions and their outcomes are in
\path{research/predictions/}. The checker of \cref{sec:gsm} is at~\cite{gsm}.
